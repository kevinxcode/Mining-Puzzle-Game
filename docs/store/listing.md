## Full description (max 4000)
Take charge of a busy open-pit mine. Every shift has a production target — and only a handful of excavators, dump trucks and haul roads to hit it.

Assign trucks to excavators, pick the fastest haul route, keep the queue moving and don't let a truck run dry halfway up the ramp. Short, satisfying puzzles that get smarter as you go.

⛏️ 60 LEVELS, 6 MINE SITES
From the Training Pit to the Iron Frontier, each region adds something new: multiple excavators, traffic and narrow roads, fuel stations, rain, road closures, breakdowns and mixed materials.

🚚 PLAN, THEN ADAPT
Drag a truck onto an excavator to reassign it, switch routes on the fly, pinch to zoom in on a busy junction. Stuck? Use a hint — or undo your last move.

⭐ EARN THREE STARS
Finish fast, keep idle time and fuel low, avoid traffic jams. Upgrade truck speed, capacity and excavator buckets, and unlock heavier equipment as you progress.

📅 FRESH CHALLENGES
A new Daily Challenge every day, a tougher Weekly Challenge, and Endless Shift mode where the target keeps rising. Share your best result with friends.

🎓 SITE INDUCTION
Learn how real haul operations work: the load–haul–dump cycle, truck matching, queues, route choice, fuel planning and site safety basics — with short quizzes, practice levels and a certificate you can export.

• Plays fully offline
• No ads, no accounts, no data collection
• Fictional sites — not affiliated with any mining company
